> "If you're nothing without the suit, then you shouldn't have it." — *Tony Stark*

## Overview

**Spider-Man: Homecoming** is a 2017 superhero movie centered on **Peter Parker**, a high school student trying to balance his normal life in Queens with his duties as the web-slinging hero *Spider-Man* under the mentorship of Tony Stark.

## Main Characters

* **Peter Parker / Spider-Man**: High school superhero trying to prove himself.
* **Adrian Toomes / The Vulture**: Former salvage company owner turned high-tech weapons dealer.
* **Ned Leeds**: Peter's loyal best friend and "guy in the chair."
* **Tony Stark / Iron Man**: Peter's mentor and suit creator.
